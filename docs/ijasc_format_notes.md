# IJASC formatting rules (extracted from IJASC_Format.doc)

Apply these when pasting `ijasc_manuscript.md` into the journal template.

## Page and layout
- Printed area 21 cm × 28 cm. Margins: 2 cm sides, 3 cm top, 2 cm bottom.
- **Conflict to resolve:** the template text says "after 'Introduction', create 2 columns,
  column width 23.04 characters, spacing 2.12 characters". Both sample papers supplied with
  the template (IJASC Vol.15 No.1, 2026) are **single column**. Confirm with the editor or
  follow the samples.

## Type
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Paper title | 15 pt Times New Roman, bold, italic, centred, line spacing 1.1 |
| Space after title | one line at 11.5 pt |
| Author names | 11.5 pt TNR, bold, centred, spacing 1.8, 11.3 pt before paragraph |
| After names | two spaces: first 11.5 pt, second 9 pt, spacing 1.1 |
| Affiliations | 10.5 pt, multiple spacing; then two spaces (10.5 pt, then 10 pt at 1.1) |
| "Abstract" heading | 11 pt TNR, bold, centred, initial capital, spacing 1.1, outline level 3; one 7 pt space after |
| Abstract body | 9 pt TNR, spacing 1.15; one blank line at 9 pt after |
| "Keywords" heading | 9 pt TNR, bold, initial capital, justified, indented 2 characters |
| Keywords | 4–6 terms, 9 pt TNR, spacing 1.1; then one 11 pt space before Introduction |
| Body text | 9.5 pt TNR, spacing 1.15, fully justified, first line indented 1 character |
| Table caption | above the table, 9 pt TNR, centred, fixed spacing 12 pt, 6 pt after |
| Table body | 9 pt TNR; leave a space after the table |
| Figure caption | below the figure, 9 pt TNR, centred, spacing 1.15; one space after the caption |
| "References" heading | 11 pt TNR, bold, ALL CAPS, fixed spacing 12 pt, centred, not numbered |
| Reference entries | 9 pt TNR, spacing 1.15, fully justified, indent 0.8 cm |

## Rules that affect the text itself
- English only. Bold and underline avoided in running text; italics allowed for emphasis and
  required for Latin/French terms (*in vitro*, *et al.*) and for symbols in equations and text.
- Headings left aligned, initial capitals; canonical section names: Abstract, Keywords,
  Introduction, Theory, Experiments, Results and Discussion, Conclusion, Acknowledgment,
  References. Numbered sections as in the sample papers.
- Equations numbered consecutively with Arabic numerals; symbols italic.
- SI units; a space between number and unit **except** °C and % (40 mg/L, 50%, 10 h).
- Figures: 600/1200 dpi; must be legible in black and white (print is greyscale, colour only
  in the electronic version). Our figures use hatching as a second encoding for this reason.
- Citations in brackets, numbered in citation order, e.g. [2,6-8]. Every listed reference must
  be cited. Journal titles abbreviated as in IEEE Abstracts.
- **"Manuscripts in which references are not in this format will be returned without review."**

## Front matter placed by the journal
Running head "International Journal of Advanced Smart Convergence Vol.x No.x x-x (20xx)",
DOI line, "IJASC xx-x-x" box, "Manuscript received / revised", corresponding author, Tel/Fax,
copyright notice.

## Submission
Signed copyright form (downloadable from the Information for Authors page) sent as a scanned
PDF to ijasc@iibc.kr; the lead author may sign for all authors.
